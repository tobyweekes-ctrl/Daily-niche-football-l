How to play

Obvious answers score low. Rare — but correct — answers score high.

  1. Every day everyone gets the same 7 football prompts.
  2. You have 25 seconds and one answer per prompt.
  3. A correct answer scores by how rare it is. A wrong answer scores 0.

Answers you submit are recorded anonymously (the text you type, nothing about you) to set the tiers from what players actually say.
